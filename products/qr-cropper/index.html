What it is

QR-Anchored Answer Sheet Cropper is a Python and OpenCV tool for scanned exams. Print a small QR on each sheet and drop in a mixed scanner batch. It identifies each page by its QR, pairs fronts and backs, corrects rotation and skew, and crops every answer cell into a clean, aligned image at about 200 dpi. It runs offline.

FAQ

How do I crop answer regions from scanned exam sheets with a QR code?

Print the QR payload on each sheet, scan the batch and run the cropper. It reads the QR in the page corners, warps each answer table to a millimetre grid and cuts every cell.

Can I sort a mixed scanner batch by student?

Yes. Pages are grouped into front/back pairs per student from their QR codes. Pages from another exam are reported as stray.

Does rotation or skew matter?

No. It handles skew and 90/180/270-degree turns.

What scan resolution do I need?

150 dpi or higher. The demo uses 200 dpi.

What do I need to run it?

Python 3.10+, opencv-python-headless and numpy on Windows, macOS or Linux. One-time purchase, full source included.

How do I crop answer boxes from scanned exams with a QR code in Python?

Print the QR on each sheet, scan the batch, and run the demo: each page is identified by its QR, deskewed to the template and cut into one image per answer cell.
