Pairy

Your computer, on your tablet.

Pairy brings your Mac's desktop, keyboard and trackpad controls to your iPad or iPhone. Touch it, type on it, and use your iPhone as the remote.

App Store ↗Pairy for Mac ↓

For iPad, iPhone and Vision Pro, with an Apple silicon Mac.

How it works

Two apps, one account. The iPad or iPhone app shows your Mac; the Mac companion shares its screen and takes your input.

  1. Get Pairy on your iPad or iPhone

    From the App Store.

  2. Install Pairy for Mac

    Download it below, then allow Screen Recording and Accessibility when macOS asks.

  3. Sign in to the same Tailscale account

    On both devices. Pairy finds your computer; with more than one, pick it by name.

What it looks like

  • Full HD streaming
  • Touch trackpad and keyboard input
  • Encrypted connections between your devices
  • An oil-painted night sky and a simple dashboard

Pairy for Mac

The companion the iPad and iPhone app connects to. Signed with our Developer ID, notarized by Apple, and downloaded straight from us.

Download Pairy for Mac

  1. Double-click the zip to unzip it, then move PairyMac.app into Applications.
  2. Open it and allow Screen Recording and Accessibility when macOS asks.
  3. Keep your Mac awake and online while you use Pairy.

To check the file, run shasum -a 256 Pairy-mac-1.4.2-142-notarized.zip and compare it with the SHA-256 here.
