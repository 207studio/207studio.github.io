2. What this site does

This site is static. It has no accounts, no login, no advertising and no analytics scripts, and it sets no cookies of its own. It is served by GitHub Pages, which keeps standard web-server logs (IP address, user agent, requested path) for security and operations under GitHub's own policy.

3. What we receive when you buy

Payment is handled by the store named on the product page — currently Polar, which is the merchant of record. We never see or store card numbers. From the store we receive the order details needed to deliver and support the product:

We do not ask for and do not want identity documents, phone numbers or addresses for a normal purchase.

4. Support email

If you write to us, we keep the message and your email address to answer you and to remember the fix. Ask us to delete it and we will, unless we must keep it for a legal or accounting reason.

5. Sharing

We do not sell data. We share only what is needed with the store that processes the payment, with our email provider, and with authorities when the law requires it.

6. Keeping and deleting

Order records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require. Support threads are deleted on request. Write to support@207studio.dev for access, correction or deletion.

7. Children

Our products are sold to adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
