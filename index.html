REMOTE DESKTOP · iPhone & iPadOn the App Store

Pairy

Formerly Padsway

Your Mac,
on your iPad.

A remote desktop app for using your Mac from your iPad. See your Mac’s screen and control it with touch, trackpad and keyboard.

What it does

  • See your Mac’s screen on your iPad
  • Control it with touch, trackpad and keyboard
  • Try the built-in demo first, no Mac needed

How to use it

  1. 1
    Install Pairy on your MacInstall and open Pairy for Mac, then allow Screen Recording and input permissions.
  2. 2
    Sign in to the same Tailscale accountUse the same account on your Mac and your tablet so the two find each other.
  3. 3
    Connect and take controlPick your Mac from the list. Its screen appears and your tablet becomes its trackpad and keyboard.

207스튜디오(207studio)는 대한민국의 초등학교 교사가 설립한 독립 소프트웨어 스튜디오입니다. 수업 준비와 판서, 평가처럼 교사의 일상에 가까운 도구를 만듭니다.
