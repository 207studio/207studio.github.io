    01Pairy

    A remote desktop app for your tablet. See your computer screen and control it with touch, trackpad and keyboard.

    For iPad, iPhone and Vision Pro owners with an Apple silicon Mac.

    • Full HD streaming

    02Chalkieboard

    An all-in-one board and lesson app for elementary classes. It is built to cover a teacher's whole lesson day in one app. It collects no personal data during class.

    For elementary school teachers who teach with an iPad.

    • Handwriting on the board, plus subject tools
    • Open lesson files, including PPTX and HWP, right in the app
    • Send the board to the classroom display with AirPlay
    Chalkieboard site ↗
  3. 03In development · WindowsCheckyBoardCheck paper exams one question at a time.

    03CheckyBoard

    A Windows app for checking paper exams one question at a time. You mark each answer right or wrong. The app handles the work around it.

    For teachers who grade paper exams.

    • Make answer sheets and split scans into a box per student and question
    • Mark one question across the whole class, then move on
    • Scores, item analysis and report cards

  7. 07Coming soon · Mac, iOS, Windows, webTtoryeotRead HWP files without 한글 installed.

    07Ttoryeot

    Ttoryeot (또렷) is our HWP/HWPX document engine, written in Rust. It opens 한글 documents fast and renders them faithfully to PDF or images. View only, no editing.

    For anyone who needs to read 한글 (HWP) files without 한글 installed.

    • Layout checked page by page against 한글 12.30 on real documents
    • Fast and light. Images in an 80-image file take 7 ms, down from 196 ms. Peak memory on a large file fell from about 1 GB to 125 MB
    • One engine for Mac, iOS, Windows and the web (WASM)
